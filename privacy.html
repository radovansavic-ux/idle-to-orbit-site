Privacy Policy

Summary: Idle to Orbit does not require an account to play. Gameplay progress is stored locally by the app. The app uses Google advertising and consent services, and Apple StoreKit for in-app purchases. Those third-party services may process data as described below.

1. Gameplay data

Idle to Orbit stores gameplay information such as progress, upgrades, settings, achievements, and similar game state locally on your device using the app’s persistence system.

The game does not require you to create an Idle to Orbit account.

2. Advertising — Google AdMob

Idle to Orbit uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to provide banner ads and optional rewarded ads.

According to Google, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process information such as IP address, device identifiers, advertising data, user interactions, crash information, and performance/diagnostic data for purposes including ad delivery, analytics, fraud prevention, and service improvement.

Actual processing can vary depending on your consent choices, region, device settings, and Google’s advertising configuration.

Google privacy information: Google Privacy Policy

3. Consent and privacy choices — Google UMP

Where required, Idle to Orbit uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and store advertising consent choices and to determine when ads may be requested.

If Google indicates that privacy options are required for your region, the app provides a Privacy Choices option in Settings so you can review or change those choices.

4. In-app purchases — Apple StoreKit

Idle to Orbit offers a non-consumable Remove Ads purchase through Apple StoreKit.

Purchase processing is handled by Apple. Idle to Orbit receives StoreKit transaction and entitlement information needed to determine whether the purchase is owned. The developer does not receive your payment card details.

Apple privacy information: Apple Privacy Policy

5. Optional rewarded ads

Rewarded ads are voluntary. Choosing to watch one may grant an in-game bonus such as temporary 2× income or doubled offline earnings after the advertising provider confirms completion.

Purchasing Remove Ads removes banner advertising, but optional rewarded ads may remain available because they provide voluntary in-game rewards.

6. Children

Idle to Orbit is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from users through an in-app account or profile. Third-party advertising and platform services may process data according to their own policies and the consent choices available in your region.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app, its third-party services, or legal requirements change. The updated date at the top of this page will be revised when material changes are made.
