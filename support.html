Remove Ads / Restore Purchases

If you previously purchased Remove Ads, open the game’s Settings screen and use Restore Purchases.

Purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. If a purchase is pending, cancelled, or temporarily unavailable, the game will not grant the ad-free entitlement until Apple confirms a verified transaction.

Rewarded ads

Rewarded ads are optional. They may be used for bonuses such as temporary 2× income or doubled offline earnings.

If a rewarded ad is unavailable, check your internet connection and try again later. Availability can also depend on the advertising provider and your region.

Privacy choices

Where required, Idle to Orbit uses Google’s consent system for advertising choices. If the option is available for your region, you can reopen it from Settings → Privacy Choices.

Common troubleshooting

  • Fully close and reopen the app.
  • Check that iOS and Idle to Orbit are up to date.
  • Check your internet connection for ads or StoreKit purchases.
  • If a purchase appears missing, use Restore Purchases.
  • When contacting support, include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened.
